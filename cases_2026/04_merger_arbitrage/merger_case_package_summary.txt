RITC 2026 — Merger Arbitrage Case (Case Package Summary)

Source: RITC 2026 Case Package (Rotman FRT-Lab). This is a paraphrased summary for team use.

1) Big picture
- Event-driven equity trading across 5 live M&A deals (10 stocks: 5 targets + 5 acquirers).
- Deal structures include all-cash, stock-for-stock, and mixed consideration.
- News updates drive changes in completion probability; targets trade as a probability-weighted blend of deal value and standalone value.
- Acquirer prices move based on deal impact (synergies, financing costs, dilution).

2) Structure / timing
- 2 heats total; each team assigns 2 members per heat (each participant trades one heat only).
- 5 independent sub-heats per heat.
- Each sub-heat = 10 minutes, representing 6 months of calendar time (120 trading days).
- News released during sub-heats.
- RIT API + RTD links enabled.

3) Tradable securities and deal terms (fixed for the sub-heat)
Deal  Target (Ticker)  Acquirer (Ticker)  Structure   Deal terms (target value)   Start price (Target / Acquirer)
D1    TGX             PHR                Cash        $50 per share               $43.70 / $47.50
D2    BYL             CLD                Stock       0.75 CLD per BYL            $43.50 / $79.30
D3    GGD             PNR                Mixed       $33 + 0.20 PNR              $31.50 / $59.80
D4    FSR             ATB                Cash        $40 per share               $30.50 / $62.20
D5    SPK             EEC                Stock       1.20 EEC per SPK            $52.80 / $48.00

4) Price logic (core mechanics)
- Target intrinsic value = p * DealValue + (1 - p) * StandaloneValue
- StandaloneValue is NOT given; infer it at t=0 using:
  StandaloneValue = (TargetStartPrice - p0 * DealValue) / (1 - p0)
- In cash deals, target price mainly moves with probability (p).
- In stock/mixed deals, target price also moves with acquirer price (hedging required).

5) News categories and probability updates
News categories:
- REG: Regulatory / antitrust
- FIN: Financing / capital markets
- SHR: Shareholder / board actions
- ALT: Strategic alternatives / competing bids
- PRC: Process / timing / conditions

Baseline impact by direction + severity (probability points):
- Positive: small +0.03, medium +0.07, large +0.14
- Negative: small -0.04, medium -0.09, large -0.18
- Ambiguous: small ±0.00–0.03, medium ±0.00–0.05, large ±0.00–0.07

Category multipliers:
- REG 1.25
- FIN 1.00
- SHR 0.90
- ALT 1.40
- PRC 0.70

Deal sensitivity multipliers:
- D1 (pharma cash): 1.00
- D2 (tech stock): 1.05
- D3 (energy mixed): 1.10
- D4 (banking cash): 1.30
- D5 (renewable stock): 1.15

Illustrative update formula:
  delta_p = base_impact * category_multiplier * deal_multiplier

6) Initial completion probabilities (p0 at t=0)
- D1 TGX/PHR: 70%
- D2 BYL/CLD: 55%
- D3 GGD/PNR: 50%
- D4 FSR/ATB: 38%
- D5 SPK/EEC: 45%

7) Trading limits, fees, close-out
- Gross limit: 100,000 shares
- Net limit: 50,000 shares
- Max order size: 5,000 shares
- Commission: $0.02 per share
- Open positions closed at end of each sub-heat at last trade prices
- If a deal resolves mid-heat, prices snap to the realized outcome

8) Objectives (as stated in package, paraphrased)
- Read news + update deal probabilities to identify mispricing in spreads
- Build positions aligned to structure (cash vs stock vs mixed), hedge acquirer exposure
- Manage risk across multiple simultaneous deals, including delays/failure risk

9) Practice vs competition
- Practice case mirrors the structure but competition may include extra news items.

10) Assessment / scoring (competition-wide context)
- RITC scoring uses ranks, not raw P&L, to reward consistency.
- Case weights: Liquidity 20%, Volatility 20%, GBE 20%, Merger Arb 20%, Algo MM 20%.
- For Merger Arb (like Liquidity + Volatility): team P&Ls summed per sub-heat, then ranked; average rank determines case rank.
- Case rank is mapped to points (higher is better), then weighted to produce final score.
- Ties broken by variance of case scores (lower variance wins).
- Top 5 awards: $5,000 / $2,500 / $1,500 / $1,000 / $500 CAD.
