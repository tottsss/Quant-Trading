RITC 2026 — GBE Energy Electricity Trading Case (Case Package Summary)

Source: RITC 2026 Case Package (Rotman FRT-Lab). Paraphrased summary for team use.

1) Big picture
- Role-based team case in a regulated electricity market.
- Produce, distribute, and trade electricity while managing penalties.

2) Structure / timing
- 2 heats; each heat has 4 sub-heats.
- Sub-heat: 15 minutes, representing 5 trading days (first week of August).
- RIT API order submission disabled; RTD enabled.
- Roles: Producer (1), Distributor (1), Traders (2).

3) Markets and instruments
- ELEC-dayX (spot): 100 MWh per contract, day-specific.
- ELEC-F (forward): 500 MWh per contract, delivers next day.
- NG (natural gas): 100 MMBtu per contract; Producers only.

4) Production and demand models
- Solar output (contracts): ELEC_solar = 6 * hours_of_sunshine. No cost.
- Gas conversion: 800 MMBtu -> 100 MWh (8 NG -> 1 ELEC-dayX).
- Distributor demand (contracts):
  Demand = 200 - 15*AT + 0.8*AT^2 - 0.01*AT^3
  AT = expected average temperature (C).
- Weather forecasts update 3x per day; final evening update is accurate.

5) Regulatory spot market (RAE)
- RAE sets spot price + volumes for ELEC-dayX; two bulletins per day.
- Spot volume is limited; forward market preferred for risk control.

6) Penalties
- Disposal penalty: $20,000 per contract for excess electricity at end of day.
- Distributor shortfall penalty: $20,000 per contract of unmet demand.

7) Limits / fees
- Max trade size: ELEC-F 10 contracts; NG 80 contracts.
- Max net positions: ELEC-dayX 300; NG 80 (Producer); ELEC-F 60.
- No transaction costs for ELEC-F and NG; ELEC-F quotes are integer prices.

8) Close-out
- ELEC-day2..day5: closed at $0; $20,000 penalty per contract for long or short.
- ELEC-day6: closed at final RAE price on day 5; no fines.

9) Objectives (paraphrased)
- Model supply/demand from news and coordinate across roles.
- Optimize production, procurement, and tender response as a team.
- Practice sessions require enough participants to be realistic.

Assessment / Scoring (competition-wide)
- Case weights: Liquidity Risk 20%, Volatility Trading 20%, GBE Electricity 20%, Merger Arbitrage 20%, Algo Market Making 20%.
- Liquidity / Volatility / Merger: team P&Ls summed per sub-heat, ranked, then average rank -> case rank.
- GBE Electricity: team members' P&Ls summed per sub-heat, ranked, then average rank -> case rank.
- Algo MM: only one team member trades; heat ranks averaged -> case rank.
- Case ranks map to points (higher is better). Final weighted score ranks teams.
- Ties are broken by variance of case scores (lower variance wins). Top 5 cash awards.
