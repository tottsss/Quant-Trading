RITC 2026 — Volatility Trading Case (Case Package Summary)

Source: RITC 2026 Case Package (Rotman FRT-Lab). Paraphrased summary for team use.

1) Big picture
- Trade RTM ETF options to exploit volatility mispricing.
- Focus on delta-hedged strategies within delta limits.

2) Structure / timing
- 2 heats; each heat has 8 sub-heats.
- Sub-heat: 5 minutes, representing 1 month (20 trading days).
- RIT API + RTD enabled.

3) Instruments
- Underlying: RTM ETF (non-dividend).
- Options: European calls/puts at strikes 45–54 (10 strikes; 20 contracts total).

4) Market dynamics
- Market maker quotes 2-cent spreads; large liquidity.
- RTM follows random walk: P_t = P_{t-1} * (1 + r_t), r_t ~ N(0, sigma).
- Options priced with Black-Scholes and zero variance risk premium.
- Analysts provide realized vol updates; market maker may lag.

5) Delta limits and penalties
- Delta limit and penalty rate announced via news each sub-heat.
- Penalty per second when |delta| exceeds limit:
  (|delta| - delta_limit) * rate

6) Limits / fees
- RTM ETF: gross 50,000 shares; net 50,000 shares; max trade size 10,000 shares.
- Options: gross 2,500 contracts; net 1,000 contracts; max trade size 100 contracts.
- Fees: $0.01 per RTM share; $1.00 per option contract.
- Each option contract represents 100 shares.

7) Close-out
- RTM closed at last traded price at end of sub-heat.
- Options cash-settled at expiration: Call max(0, S-K); Put max(0, K-S).

8) Objectives (paraphrased)
- Forecast volatility from news; identify mispriced options.
- Use Greeks to manage exposure and hedge delta.

Assessment / Scoring (competition-wide)
- Case weights: Liquidity Risk 20%, Volatility Trading 20%, GBE Electricity 20%, Merger Arbitrage 20%, Algo Market Making 20%.
- Liquidity / Volatility / Merger: team P&Ls summed per sub-heat, ranked, then average rank -> case rank.
- GBE Electricity: team members' P&Ls summed per sub-heat, ranked, then average rank -> case rank.
- Algo MM: only one team member trades; heat ranks averaged -> case rank.
- Case ranks map to points (higher is better). Final weighted score ranks teams.
- Ties are broken by variance of case scores (lower variance wins). Top 5 cash awards.
