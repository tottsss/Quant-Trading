RITC 2026 — Algorithmic Market Making Case (Case Package Summary)

Source: RITC 2026 Case Package (Rotman FRT-Lab). Paraphrased summary for team use.

1) Big picture
- Algorithmic market making in four stocks under position limits.
- Provide liquidity, manage inventory risk, and adapt to news-driven volatility.

2) Structure / timing
- 12 heats; 1 team member trades all heats.
- Each heat: 5 minutes, representing one trading week (5 days).
- Algorithmic trading only; no manual trading during heats.
- 2 minutes between heats to reload algorithm.
- RIT API + RTD enabled.

3) Instruments
- Stocks: SPNG, SMMR, ATMN, WNTR (CAD), start price $25 each.
- Market order fee: $0.02 per share.
- Passive rebates: SPNG 0.01; SMMR 0.02; ATMN 0.015; WNTR 0.025.
- Max order size: 10,000 shares.

4) Aggregate position limit
- Limit announced at start of each heat.
- Penalty: $10 per share above limit, assessed each minute.

5) Additional trading limits
- Gross / net position limits apply; penalty $5 per share for exceeding.

6) Close-out
- All positions closed at end of heat at last traded price.

7) Objectives (paraphrased)
- Quote both sides to earn spread while avoiding excessive inventory.
- Manage aggregate exposure and flatten near limits.

Assessment / Scoring (competition-wide)
- Case weights: Liquidity Risk 20%, Volatility Trading 20%, GBE Electricity 20%, Merger Arbitrage 20%, Algo Market Making 20%.
- Liquidity / Volatility / Merger: team P&Ls summed per sub-heat, ranked, then average rank -> case rank.
- GBE Electricity: team members' P&Ls summed per sub-heat, ranked, then average rank -> case rank.
- Algo MM: only one team member trades; heat ranks averaged -> case rank.
- Case ranks map to points (higher is better). Final weighted score ranks teams.
- Ties are broken by variance of case scores (lower variance wins). Top 5 cash awards.
