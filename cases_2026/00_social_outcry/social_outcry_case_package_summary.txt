RITC 2026 — Social Outcry Case (Case Package Summary)

Source: RITC 2026 Case Package (Rotman FRT-Lab). Paraphrased summary for team use.

1) Big picture
- Open-outcry futures trading to break the ice; manual trading.
- This case does NOT count toward final competition scoring.

2) Structure / timing
- One 30-minute trading heat.
- Participants start with a neutral futures position.

3) Market dynamics
- Instrument: RT100 index futures.
- Futures price set by transactions; spot price follows a stochastic path influenced by qualitative news.
- News can move spot immediately or with lags.

4) Limits / fees
- Max 5 contracts per ticket.
- Contract multiplier: 10.
- No commissions; no net position limits.

5) Settlement and scoring (case only)
- All trades settle at the closing spot price.
- P&L ranking based on total session P&L.

6) Rules / conduct
- Verbal commitments are binding.
- Tickets must be complete, legible, and delivered (white copy) to market agents.
- English only; staff can break unreasonable trades.
- Brief tutorial/demo before the session.

Assessment / Scoring (competition-wide)
- Case weights: Liquidity Risk 20%, Volatility Trading 20%, GBE Electricity 20%, Merger Arbitrage 20%, Algo Market Making 20%.
- Liquidity / Volatility / Merger: team P&Ls summed per sub-heat, ranked, then average rank -> case rank.
- GBE Electricity: team members' P&Ls summed per sub-heat, ranked, then average rank -> case rank.
- Algo MM: only one team member trades; heat ranks averaged -> case rank.
- Case ranks map to points (higher is better). Final weighted score ranks teams.
- Ties are broken by variance of case scores (lower variance wins). Top 5 cash awards.
